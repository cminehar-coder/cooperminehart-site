How it works

The score

Each dimension gets scaled 0-100 between fixed endpoints (below), capped at the ends, then weighted. The endpoints are my picks, not standards. Filtering never rescales a score.

Score = Σ (weight × dimension score)
Dimension0 points100 points

Default weights: clearance 30%, approach 20%, departure 20%, breakover 20%, tire 10%, wheelbase 0% (it's already in breakover).

What it misses

It's static geometry only. No traction, articulation, tread, gearing, armor, payload, reliability, price, or desert-running. Use the hardware filter for low range and lockers.

Tire diameter = rim + 2 × width × aspect ratio (flotation sizes like 37x12.50R17 are just 37 in).

Missing breakover gets estimated as 2 × atan(2 × clearance ÷ wheelbase). That assumes the low point is centered, so it's rough. Turn it off to only use published angles. Vehicles without published approach/departure angles aren't included.

Sources

MPG is EPA combined, matched by year, engine, transmission and trim. It's a lab number. Manufacturers measure clearance differently, and tires, load, air suspension and hitches all change it. Every vehicle links its sources.
