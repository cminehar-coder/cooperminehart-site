How it works

1. Usable vs. gross kWh

Net usable energy on Y: The vertical kWh axis plots net usable battery capacity rather than nominal gross pack size so conservative top/bottom buffers (for example, Porsche Taycan at 97 kWh usable vs. 105 kWh gross) do not inflate a car's vertical position. Gross capacity and buffer percentage are shown alongside usable kWh in the inspector card and CSV export.

Where net kWh is estimated: When an automaker only publishes nominal pack kWh (common in China MIIT filings), usable capacity is estimated from teardown or EV Database discharge data.

EPA verification: All 100 light-duty US configurations match the DOE/EPA FuelEconomy.gov vehicles.csv release; Class 2b pickups above 8,500 lb GVWR (Hummer EV, Escalade IQ) use OEM ratings run on the EPA test procedure.

2. Regional MSRPs, FX, and VAT

Local window stickers: Every row stores local-currency base retail MSRPs for each country where that pack is currently offered for sale, excluding destination charges and purchase subsidies/tax credits. Selecting one country plots its exact local sticker; selecting multiple markets plots the lowest purchasable MSRP in the active set. Note that GM's Silverado EV Work Truck (WT) entries are commercial fleet trims occasionally sold off dealer lots; retail Custom and LT trims start higher.

Consumption tax normalization: US and Canadian MSRPs are quoted pre-tax, whereas European (19-20% VAT), Chinese (13% VAT), and Japanese/Korean/Australian (10% GST/VAT) stickers include tax (Norway exempts BEVs up to 500,000 NOK). Check Strip embedded VAT / GST for pre-tax comparisons.

Spot FX (Nov 2026 approx.): 1 USD = 1.39 CAD · 0.925 EUR · 0.785 GBP · 10.95 NOK · 7.24 CNY · 1,390 KRW · 152 JPY · 1.52 AUD.

3. Cell chemistries and exclusions

Alongside High-Nickel (NMC, NCA, NCMA) and LFP (Blade 1.0/2.0, Shenxing, Golden Brick), three post-LFP chemistries are in retail showrooms as of late 2026:

  • LMFP / M3P: Manganese-doped phosphate (~4.1 V plateau) in Luxeed S7/R7 and Exeed Sterra ES/ET.
  • Sodium-Ion: Zero-lithium packs in Changan Nevo A06 (CATL Naxtra 175 Wh/kg), JAC Yiwei 3, and JMEV EV3.
  • Semi-Solid State: QingTao oxide-polymer hybrid electrolytes in MG4 Anxin Semi-Solid (53.95 kWh) and IM L6 Max Lightyear (123.7 kWh net / 133 kWh gross). NIO's 150 kWh WeLion pack is excluded because it is rental-only via BaaS Power Swap.
Discontinued, withdrawn, or rental-only packs excluded (11)

    4. Test-cycle range equivalency (EPA vs. WLTP, CLTC, Japan WLTC, and Korea MOTIE)

    The exact same EV battery pack receives very different official range numbers across regions because laboratory chassis-dynamometer cycles run at different speeds and apply different post-test derating rules. Aerodynamic drag power scales with the cube of speed (Pdrag ∝ v3), so low-speed urban cycles with frequent regenerative braking and long idle pauses produce much higher range figures than high-speed motorway cycles or sub-freezing cabin-heat tests.

    Whenever a vehicle has an official US EPA rating, the chart plots that exact EPA number with a solid marker. Only when a configuration is not sold in North America does the default Harmonize WLTP / CLTC to EPA-eq. toggle convert its local homologation figure into approximate EPA-equivalent miles (marked with a hollow white center dot on the chart):
